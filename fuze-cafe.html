02 / Logo guidelines

Keep the current FUZE Cafe logo clear, legible and consistent across every application. These examples use the approved current logo, not the retired mark shown in the older guidelines.

01 / Logo safe area

Maintain clear space on all four sides of the logo. No typography, imagery, borders or other elements should enter this area.
